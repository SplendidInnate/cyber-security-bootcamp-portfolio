My Favourite Hobbies

Playing PC Games

I spent almost every afternoon playing games such as ETS 2, GTA, Hitman, and Need for Speed.

Car Spotting

I enjoyed sitting by the road with friends, identifying performance cars by their sound and appearance.

My Favourite Toys

Toy Cars

I had a large collection of toy cars and built racing tracks using cardboard boxes.

Toy Guns

My friends and I played action games in the backyard for hours on end.

Fun Fact

These childhood hobbies helped develop my love for technology, gaming, and automobiles.
