My Childhood

Hobbies:

  • Playing PC Games

    - I spent almost every afternoon playing PC games like ETS 2, GTA, Hitman, NFS: Most Wanted and other PC games till late.

    - I used to spend hours mastering difficult levels, which is probably where my interest in tech first started.

  • Car spotting

    - I would spend my weekends on the streets and sitting with my friends by the main road which led to the Swaziland Border, trying to identify performance cars.

    - I was always fascinated by the sound of a high-revving engine and would try to guess the car before it even came into view.

Toys:

  • Toy Cars

    - I had a massive collection of small cars that I would line up across the living room floor in a perfectly straight row.

    - I remember creating imaginary race tracks out of cardboard boxes to see which of my cars was the fastest.

  • Toy Guns

    - My friends and I would spend the whole afternoon running around the backyard playing action games with our toy guns.

    We would stay outside until it was dark, pretending we were on top-secret missions around the house.
